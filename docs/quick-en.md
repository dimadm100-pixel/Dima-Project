# Quick reference — what each page does

Five main pages sit in the bottom bar; the rest are under **More** (⋯).

**Bottom bar**

- **Position** — what actually happened. The balance of every card and your cash, plus the full ledger of money in and out.
- **Cash Flow** — the plan. Enter expected income and expenses by date, then compare PLAN against FACT.
- **Home** — the overview. How much you have, how long it lasts, recent activity, alerts, goal progress.
- **Planner** — builds next year's budget automatically from how you actually spend. Free, works offline.
- **Insights** — analysis of your own numbers: where money leaks, when each goal lands.

**Under More (⋯)**

- **FX Rates** — official Central Bank rates (USD, EUR), with trend and history.
- **Assistant** — the in-app AI. Needs an Anthropic API key; everything else works without it.
- **Search** — find any transaction or budget line.
- **Acc. Cash** — day by day: how much you should have per the budget, and how much you really have.
- **P&L** — monthly income, expenses and net profit, with a breakdown by category.
- **Balance** — assets, liabilities, and net worth.
- **Goals** — three goals: Marriage, Home, Umrah, each with its own calculator.
- **Targets** — the dates you care about: how much net cash you wanted by then, and how much you had.
- **Specs** — what a single figure is made of. "Food 200,000" → bread 20,000, oil 30,000…
- **Credit** — log your credit score over time and watch the trend.
- **Settings** — opening balance, recurring payments, API key, and **Export (backup)**.

**At the top** — the 💰 Finance ⇄ 🗓️ Personal switcher. Personal is not about money: today's plan, tomorrow's, your weekly routine and deadlines.

---

**The three things that matter most**

1. The **+** button adds income or an expense. It asks which account it came from.
2. All data stays **on this phone only**. Nothing is uploaded, nobody else can see it.
3. So press **Settings → Export data** now and then and keep the file. There is no other backup.
