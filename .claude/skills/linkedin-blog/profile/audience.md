# Beats

Topics that are in scope, most important first. These drive the weekly search.

1. Global macro and markets, read through their effect on companies - financing
   costs, liquidity, working capital, operating cash flow quality
2. Inflation and consumer economics, especially where it shows up in prices
   people in Tashkent actually pay
3. AI and technology as they change finance work, analysis and competitiveness
4. Uzbekistan and Central Asia economy - Central Bank rate decisions, the soum,
   banking sector, IMF and World Bank assessments, foreign investment
5. Corporate finance and reporting practice - IFRS changes, what strong and weak
   results actually look like
6. Personal finance for young professionals - budgeting, emergency funds, credit,
   starting to invest
7. The finance career track itself - ACCA, exams, skills worth building

**Adjacent topics** worth using in a thin news week:
- Commodity and currency moves that affect Uzbekistan (gold, gas, cotton, the
  soum against the dollar and the rouble)
- Regional trade and logistics, remittance flows from Russia

**Out of scope**
- Party politics, geopolitics except where it moves markets or costs
- Generic leadership, hiring and motivational content
- Anything the author has no professional standing to claim

# Sources

Worth searching directly each week.

- Central Bank of Uzbekistan (cbu.uz) - rate decisions, inflation prints, banking
  statistics
- Statistics Agency of Uzbekistan (stat.uz)
- Local business press: Spot.uz, Kun.uz, Gazeta.uz, Review.uz
- IMF and World Bank country and regional publications, EBRD Transition reports
- Reuters, Financial Times, Bloomberg for global macro
- ACCA Global newsroom and IFRS Foundation for standards and profession news
- Earnings and results from large regional and global companies where the numbers
  illustrate a point about cash flow or margin

# Recurring angles

Positions the author has already taken and would take again. Keep the weekly
throughline consistent with these.

- Uncertainty itself, not bad news, is what markets and companies struggle with.
  "Bozorlar yomon xabarlarni qabul qila oladi. Eng katta xavf esa — noaniqlik."
- The strongest companies now are not the fastest growing but the ones combining
  financial discipline, adaptability and technology
- The shift from growth at any cost to sustainable, resilient growth
- Investors have moved from rewarding revenue growth to rewarding operating cash
  flow quality, stable profitability and working capital efficiency
- AI is becoming a real competitive advantage rather than a trend, but only where
  it is integrated into analytics, operations and decision-making
- Treat yourself like a small company: run your own P&L and cash flow
- Be conservative - overestimate costs, underestimate income
- Invest first and spend what remains, rather than the reverse
