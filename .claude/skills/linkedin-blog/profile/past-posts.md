## Post 2 - approx. 1 year ago - English - shrinkflation explainer, with image

How Businesses Fight Inflation: The Case of Shrinkflation

Inflation does not only challenge consumers, it also puts businesses under
serious pressure. Rising costs of raw materials, logistics, and wages force
companies to adapt. One common strategy is shrinkflation - keeping the same price
but reducing the quantity or portion size.

🔸 Example:
KFC's lunchboxes in Uzbekistan are still priced at around 30,000 sums, just like
before. Yet, the portion sizes have significantly decreased. The price tag feels
familiar, but the value received is smaller.

✅ Advantages for Businesses
Prices appear stable, maintaining affordability in the eyes of customers.
Demand is less likely to drop compared to outright price increases.
Businesses can survive without shocking consumers with frequent price changes.

❌ Disadvantages for Customers (and Businesses)
Customers eventually notice the smaller portions, which may create frustration.
Brand loyalty and trust can erode over time.
Competitors who offer better value may attract disappointed customers.

Who Can Use Shrinkflation?
- Food & Beverage (restaurants, snacks, fast food, packaged goods) - portions or
package sizes can be reduced.
- Consumer Goods (detergents, cosmetics, household items) - bottles, packs, or
rolls can become smaller while price stays the same.

Who Cannot Use Shrinkflation?
- Services (education, healthcare, consultancy, transport) - reducing quality or
time of service is quickly noticed and often unacceptable.
- Durables & Tech (electronics, furniture, appliances) - customers expect clear
specifications; reducing features or quality while keeping the same price harms
reputation immediately.
- Luxury goods - brand value depends on prestige and experience, so cutting back
risks damaging brand image.

In the short term, shrinkflation helps companies manage inflation. In the long
run, however, it raises the question of how businesses can balance cost
sustainability with customer trust.

Do you think shrinkflation is a fair strategy, or does it harm consumer
confidence too much?

## Post 4 - approx. 1 year ago - English - SSI explainer, with image

What is SSI?

SSI (Social Selling Index) is a score from LinkedIn (0 to 100) that shows how
effectively you're using LinkedIn to build your brand and connect with others. It
breaks down into 4 parts:
- Build your brand - Is your profile strong and do you post useful content?
- Find the right people - Are you connecting with relevant professionals?
- Engage with insights - Do you comment, share, or post thoughtful things?
- Build relationships - Are you building real connections, not just adding people?

📌 How relevant is it?
It's especially useful if you're:
- In sales, marketing, recruiting, or looking for new opportunities.
- Trying to grow your personal brand or professional network.
For example:
- A finance student sharing insights about budgeting and connecting with CFOs
will boost SSI.
- A recruiter messaging candidates and commenting on HR posts will score high.

📊 What does it tell you?
- Where you're strong (e.g., profile looks great, people engage with your posts).
- Where you need to improve (e.g., not connecting with relevant people).
- How you compare to others in your industry and network.

Think of it like your LinkedIn health check - the higher your score, the more
visibility, trust, and opportunities you're likely to get.

So, what's your SSI score? What's your strongest side?

## Post 6 - recent - Uzbek with English version in comments - personal finance

💡 20–30 yosh oralig'idagi eng keng tarqalgan moliyaviy xatolar — va ularni
qanday oldini olish mumkin (English version is in the comments section)

20–30 yoshda pulni boshqarish murakkab tuyuladi — yangi daromad, yangi
majburiyatlar va cheksiz tanlovlar. Ammo aynan shu yillarda sizning moliyaviy
odatlaringiz kelajagingizni shakllantira boshlaydi.
Quyida men ko'rgan (va ba'zilarini o'zim ham qilgan) eng keng tarqalgan xatolar
hamda ularni tuzatish bo'yicha tavsiyalar:

1️⃣ Byudjetsiz yashash
Ko'p odamlar pulining qayerga ketayotganini bilmay sarf qilishadi.
 Oddiy oylik byudjet — hatto Excel yoki Google Sheetsda — tartib va nazorat
beradi.
 50/30/20 qoidasini sinab ko'ring: 50% zaruratlar, 30% istaklar, 20% jamg'arma
va investitsiyalar.
O'zingizni kichik kompaniya deb biling — oylik Daromad va Xarajat (P&L) hamda
Pul oqimi (Cash Flow) jadvalini tuzing (hatto kundalik xarajat va daromadlarni
yozish ham yetarli).
 Agar siz moliya sohasi bilan shug'ullansangiz, Balans jadvali — bonus. Ishoning,
bu sizga ko'proq tushuncha beradi.

2️⃣ Favqulodda jamg'armani e'tiborsiz qoldirish
Kutilmagan holatlar — sog'lig'ingiz bilan bog'liq xarajatlar yoki kundalik
buyumlarning shikastlanishi — bir necha oylik mehnatingizni yo'qqa chiqarishi
mumkin.
Kamida 3–6 oylik zaruriy xarajatlaringizni qoplashga yetadigan favqulodda
jamg'arma yarating.
Bu shunchaki zaxira emas — bu tinchlik manbai.

3️⃣ Kreditni noto'g'ri ishlatish

emas.
Ular vosita, ammo noto'g'ri ishlatilsa xavfli.
Har oy to'liq to'lang, va qaytarishga ishonchingiz yo'q bo'lgan narsani sotib
olmang. Aks holda, bir qarzni yopish uchun boshqasini olish zanjiriga tushib

4️⃣ Investitsiyani kechiktirish
